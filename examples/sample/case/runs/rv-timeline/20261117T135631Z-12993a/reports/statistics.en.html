Limitations

  • Times recorded without a time zone are placed as if they were UTC and shown as written; they are never converted, so their true instant may differ by up to 14 hours.
  • Cross-source comparisons tolerate 300 s of difference; smaller disagreements between clocks are not reported.
  • In this version the only sources are the audited outputs of Rakvor tool runs in the case folder; times those tools did not extract (other logs, other devices) are not in the timeline.
  • Each reader supports the tool output versions it was written for; runs of other versions are left out and listed, never guessed.
  • A timeline without signals does not prove that the times are true; it shows that the sources read agree within the tolerance.
  • A trusted timestamp proves the audit log existed in this exact form at the stated time; it does not prove that the examined copy matches the original source.
